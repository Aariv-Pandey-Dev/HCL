Grocery Tracker helps a household keep track of the groceries it buys and when they expire, so less food is wasted. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

Information stored on your phone

The groceries you add (names, quantities, prices, purchase and expiry dates), your plan and your kitchen settings are saved only on your device. We do not run a server that stores them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

The AI assistant (Paid plan)

When you use an AI feature, the app sends only what that feature needs to Google’s Gemini model through Google Firebase AI Logic: the order text or email you chose to import, a photo you chose to scan, or the list of groceries you picked for a recipe. Google processes this to produce the answer. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Gmail access (optional, Paid plan)

If you tap “Connect email”, the app asks Google for read-only access to your Gmail. It searches only for order confirmation emails from online grocery and shopping stores from the last 30 days, reads those messages on your phone, and sends their text to the AI assistant to find the groceries. It never sends, deletes or changes email, and the access token stays on your device for at most one hour.

Grocery Tracker’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Email data is used only to add groceries to your list, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not read by people.

Camera and photos

Photos you take or choose (a product label or an order screenshot) are used only to read dates or products for that one request. They are not kept by the app.

Payments

This version of the app does not take payments. The checkout screen is a demonstration: card numbers typed into it are never saved or sent anywhere.

Children

The app is designed for families. It does not ask for anyone’s age, name or contact details and shows no advertising.

Your choices

  • Use the free plan without any AI or email features.
  • Switch to a different Gmail account, or disconnect Gmail, from the Orders tab (Change account / Disconnect). Disconnecting hands the app’s access back to Google.
  • Remove Gmail access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete all your data from Plan → Data → Clear everything, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
